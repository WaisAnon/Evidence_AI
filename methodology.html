HOW EVIDENCE AI WORKS

Methodology

Evidence AI is an evidence navigator, not a truth machine.

Core method

  1. Define the question and avoid changing its meaning.
  2. Separate direct evidence from indirect evidence and interpretation.
  3. Present material evidence that supports and challenges the claim.
  4. State uncertainty, limitations, and missing information.
  5. Prefer primary and authoritative sources when live retrieval is available.
  6. Never treat model agreement as proof.

Source treatment

An official source establishes what an institution published or stated. It does not automatically prove every allegation inside that source. Academic papers are assessed by study design, relevance, replication, sample size, and limitations rather than prestige alone.

Current beta limitation

The free beta can generate AI-assisted research starting points without live retrieval. Reports must therefore disclose when sources were not directly retrieved. Users should verify consequential claims using primary material.

Independence

Evidence AI is independently operated and is not affiliated with, funded by, sponsored by, or managed by any government or political organization.

Corrections and challenges

Evidence AI should allow users to submit missing evidence and challenge a conclusion. Future report versions should retain transparent correction histories.
