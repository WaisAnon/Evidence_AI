Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2026

Information processed

Questions submitted to Evidence AI are sent to our server-side AI endpoint and may be processed by GroqCloud or Google Gemini. Do not submit confidential information, passwords, health records, financial details, or sensitive personal data.

Rate limiting

Cloudflare may provide the visitor IP address to the server. Evidence AI converts it into a temporary hashed identifier for abuse prevention. Hourly counters expire automatically and the plain IP address is not stored in the rate-limit database.

Browser storage

We use local browser storage only to remember that you have seen the privacy notice. We do not currently use advertising cookies.

Accounts and analytics

The current beta does not include user accounts or permanent saved investigations. This notice must be updated before accounts, analytics, email notifications, or payments are introduced.

Third parties

The service uses Cloudflare for hosting and security, GroqCloud and Google Gemini for AI processing, and may retrieve public information from external sources in future versions.

Contact

Until a dedicated Evidence AI support address is published, contact details will be provided through the official Evidence AI social profile or website update.
