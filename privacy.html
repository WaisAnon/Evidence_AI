Privacy

This page explains what information Evidence AI processes, why it is processed, which service providers are involved, and the choices available to users.

← Back to Evidence AI

Overview

Evidence AI is designed to collect as little personal information as reasonably possible. Users can access the public website and ask questions without creating an account.

Evidence AI does not intentionally require a name, email address, phone number, physical address, or government identification number to use its research tools.

Do not submit sensitive information.

Do not enter passwords, authentication codes, financial information, medical information, private communications, confidential workplace information, personal addresses, phone numbers, government identifiers, stolen records, or unlawfully obtained personal data.

Questions are sent to external artificial-intelligence providers for processing. Treat the question box as a public research tool, not as a confidential communication channel.

Information processed when you use the website

Questions and prompts

When you use Investigate or Search DOJ Records, the text of your question is processed to generate an assessment.

Evidence AI does not currently operate a user account system or intentionally save questions in a permanent Evidence AI database. Hosting and AI service providers may process requests, operational metadata, and temporary logs according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Technical information

Cloudflare may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Request date and time
  • Requested page or API route
  • Security and performance information
  • Error and diagnostic information

Rate-limit information

Evidence AI limits the number of AI requests that one visitor can make during an hour. This protects the free service from abuse and helps keep it available to other users.

For rate limiting, the website temporarily processes the visitor’s IP address and browser user-agent information. That combination is converted into an anonymous cryptographic identifier before being stored in Cloudflare Workers KV.

The plain IP address is not intentionally stored in the Evidence AI rate-limit database. The temporary counter is configured to expire automatically after approximately two hours.

How information is used

Information is processed for the following purposes:

  • Answering user questions
  • Searching public DOJ press releases
  • Generating structured evidence assessments
  • Displaying relevant official source links
  • Applying hourly request limits
  • Detecting security threats and misuse
  • Diagnosing errors and maintaining the website
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

Evidence AI does not currently sell personal information or use questions for targeted advertising.

Service providers

Cloudflare

Cloudflare provides website hosting, server-side functions, security services, and temporary rate-limit storage. Cloudflare may process IP addresses, request headers, security information, and other operational metadata needed to deliver and protect the service.

GroqCloud

GroqCloud is the primary AI provider. Questions and associated research instructions may be sent to GroqCloud to generate an assessment.

Groq states that inference inputs and outputs are not retained by default, although information may be retained temporarily for platform reliability, security, abuse investigation, or where a feature requires storage.

Google Gemini

Google Gemini is used as a backup AI provider when the primary provider is unavailable. A question may therefore be sent to Google for processing.

Evidence AI currently uses the free Gemini developer service. Content submitted to free Gemini services may be used by Google to improve its products and may be reviewed by authorized human reviewers under Google’s applicable terms.

For this reason, users must not submit personal, sensitive, confidential, proprietary, or legally privileged information.

U.S. Department of Justice

DOJ search terms may be sent to the publicly available U.S. Department of Justice press-release service. Evidence AI uses the returned public records to generate DOJ-grounded assessments and display official source links.

Cookies and local tracking

Evidence AI does not currently use advertising cookies, behavioral advertising trackers, or a user-account cookie.

Cloudflare may use strictly necessary security technologies to protect the website, detect automated traffic, or maintain service reliability.

If analytics, advertising, membership, or additional cookie- based features are introduced later, this notice will be updated and consent controls will be added where required.

Retention

Evidence AI keeps its own data collection limited. Temporary rate-limit counters are configured to expire after approximately two hours.

Technical logs and request metadata may be retained by Cloudflare, GroqCloud, Google, or other infrastructure providers for periods determined by their applicable terms, security requirements, account settings, and legal obligations.

Security

Evidence AI uses HTTPS, server-side API keys, Cloudflare security infrastructure, request limits, and restricted source-link handling to reduce security and privacy risks.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should avoid submitting information that could cause harm if disclosed, retained, or reviewed by a service provider.

Children

Evidence AI is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Users under 18 should use the service only with appropriate guidance from a parent or lawful guardian.

Children must not submit personal information about themselves or another person.

External links

Evidence AI links to external websites, including official DOJ pages. External websites operate under their own privacy, security, and content policies.

Evidence AI is not responsible for the data practices or availability of an external website.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, users may request information about personal data processed by Evidence AI, ask for a correction or deletion, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, or raise a privacy grievance.

Because Evidence AI does not currently require an account and uses anonymous rate-limit identifiers, it may not always be possible to connect temporary technical information to a particular user.
